Franchise Hockey Manager Help & Tips

{{ config.LEAGUE_DISPLAY_NAME }} · practical in-game guidance for GMs

This guide consolidates core FHM screens and workflows so you can manage rosters, lines, tactics, development, chemistry, and finances with more confidence. Use it alongside your league site tools (trades, AP, staff, and operations).

Quick-start checklist

  1. Review your roster and dress the right players for the next game.
  2. Confirm your schedule and upcoming opponents.
  3. Set lines and tactics (or use assistant tools carefully).
  4. Assign training programs early in preseason.
  5. Monitor team harmony and finances monthly.

Multiplayer Checklist

  1. Login your team and import from the league's server.
  2. Make changes to your team (roster, lineups, injuries, tactics, finances, etc.).
  3. Save your game locally.
  4. Export to the league server.

Roster

The Roster screen is your overview of every player on a team. Use it when you need general information about your roster or any opponent.

The Roster Overview has up to eight tabs (not all may be active depending on league rules):

  • Active Roster Players available for games and line/tactic editing.
  • Stats Current-season statistics shortcut.
  • Contracts All signed players, regardless of current club.
  • Protected List Owned rights, signed or unsigned.
  • Injury List Injured reserve.
  • Waivers Players currently on waivers.
  • Trading Block Publicly listed trade candidates.
  • Reserve List Storage when no farm team exists (common in historical/fictional modes).

Most columns sort by clicking the header. Click a player for his profile, or right-click for context actions. Use FHM Views and Filters to narrow the list.

Right-click options

Commands vary by tab—for example, dress/scratch on Active Roster, or abandon rights on Protected List.

Lineups

Open Lineups from the team menu to plan dressed lists and priority slots for even-strength and special teams (plus goalies, extra attackers, and shootouts).

If lists are empty, the game behaves normally. Use Clear Lineups on the Action menu to deactivate the feature. For lineups to apply, set Setting Lineups and Tactics in Manager Settings to your assistant—you are overriding the assistant’s default logic.

When the Even Strength tab has entries, the AI clears line data before each game and:

  • Fills dressed list and line slots by listed priority.
  • Builds units in C/LW/RW/LD/RD order using priority lists.
  • Dresses and locks each placed player to that spot.
  • Fills remaining open spots with standard AI lineup logic.

Tip: Adding even one player to Even Strength activates Lineups for that spot while AI handles the rest—useful for partial control without full manual line building.

Create Lines from Lineups

On the Lines screen—preview what the AI will do without playing a game.

AI Set Lineups

Fills lists by crude talent sort. Shuffle afterward; don’t use the raw order as-is.

Clear Lineups

Removes all entries and disables lineup override behavior.

Unit-level tactics remain in place regardless of which players Lineups assigns to a line.

Schedule

The Schedule screen lists every game for the season—played and upcoming. Playoff games appear when matchups are set.

  • Click a final score to open the boxscore.
  • Click a team name to jump to that roster.
  • Use Change View to switch between calendar and list layouts.

Lines

On the Lines screen you set combinations used in games. In GM-only mode your head coach controls lines and you cannot edit them.

The Dressed List (left) shows who is in the game lineup. Right-click to scratch or dress players. Happiness, fitness, season rating, and ability are shown; use the dropdown for detail.

Drag players into line slots on the right, or check a player on the dressed list then click his destination. Lock a player to a line with the checkbox beside his name—AI line setup respects locks, but AI Setup Organization will break them.

Four tabs cover 5-on-5, power play, penalty kill, and 4-on-4/3-on-3 units.

The Goalie Starts tab defines how the AI picks starters. Enable Use goalie start settings to override default selection; lock settings to stop the AI from changing them later.

Tactics

Tactics has three tabs—Global, Unit, and Player—so you can coach top-down or micromanage individuals.

  • Global — team-wide settings and preferences.
  • Unit — override global settings per 5-man unit; star ratings show relative strength.
  • Player — individual tactical tendencies and roles (settings stay at unit/global level).

Global preferences

  • Pull Goalie (down 1 / down 2) — seconds remaining before pulling for an extra attacker.
  • Goalie Switch — how quickly the AI pulls a struggling starter.
  • Offensive / Defensive Zone Faceoff Priority — which lines get priority by zone.
  • Line Matching — preferred opponent line matchups (easier at home with last change).

Tactical settings (by situation)

Offensive even strength

Breakout, neutral zone offence, attacking zone offence.

Defensive even strength

Forechecking, neutral zone coverage, defensive zone coverage.

Power play

Breakout, attacking zone offence, defensive approach when possession is lost.

Penalty killing

Forechecking, neutral zone coverage, approach after winning the puck.

Coaches have preferred tactics—they use and execute those settings more effectively.

Player Fits below each setting lists who suits the selected tendency well or poorly.

Training

Training Programs (preseason) and Targeted Training (in-season skill focus) guide player development.

Program types

  • Skill-specific — one skill; slowest but most precise.
  • Skill group — three related skills; medium speed.
  • Skill category — broad category; faster than group training.
  • General — fastest, no control over which skill improves.

Progress & timing

Assign programs between July 7 and the last preseason day—later starts are less effective and riskier. Non-general slots are limited (often 5–10, up to ~14 with strong coaches). Players can only switch programs while on General Training.

Progress runs 0→100 from games, development camp, preseason days, and slow off-season gains. Hitting 100 before preseason ends grants an immediate +1 and ends the program. If incomplete at preseason end, improvement chance equals progress%; failure can rarely reduce a targeted attribute.

Targeted training

Pick a specific skill (or General) per category column. Targeting slows development slightly—more so above 15—and heavily favors that skill on improvement rolls. Attributes already at 20+ cannot be targeted.

Team harmony

Team Harmony tracks personalities, conflicts, cliques, and happiness.

High-controversy players generate more Incidents, especially when the GM has low discipline. Incidents create Conflicts that lower harmony and happiness.

Cliques form as teammates take sides, escalating future incidents. Conflicts and cliques fade over time (faster with good Likability and Professionalism) or end when you trade, transfer, or release involved players—farm assignments only slightly mitigate conflict.

GM tip: Address locker-room problems before they compound. One disruptive veteran can drag down multiple lines worth of morale.

Finances

Annual finances (July 1)

Adjust long-term settings once per year on July 1 (frozen after advancing a day):

  • Ticket prices — affects revenue, attendance, and fan loyalty.
  • Arena improvements — one-time spend for seating or premium conversions.
  • New arena — major capital project (player-initiated only).
  • Wage budget — player payroll cap; also drives staff budget.
  • Transfer budget — cash purchase limit in applicable leagues.
  • Player development / youth system — development bonus and junior attraction.
  • Strategic marketing — fan loyalty investment.
  • Owner profit-taking — owner may skim surplus based on patience rating.

Enable Assistant in Team Control Settings for AI finance management (AI will not build a new arena).

Monthly finances

Calculated on the first of each month. Revenue streams include game revenue (season tickets and luxury boxes announced seven days before the regular season), broadcast shares, sponsorship/merchandising, owner contributions, and transfer income. Expenses include fixed costs and payroll.

Monthly operations budget

Editable on the first of each month (settings carry forward unless changed):

Scouting support

Funds travel and operations; adds up to +5 scouting strength beyond staff ratings.

Team morale

Road-trip and facility quality—happiness bonus/penalty for active and IR players.

Promotions & arena ops

Marketing and in-game experience spending to boost home attendance.

Payroll and starting funds are informational—change them by altering roster or staff, not by editing the finance screen directly.
